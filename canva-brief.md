# Canva Design Brief — KEACH Pitch Deck

Create a 12-slide 16:9 startup/education pitch deck titled **KEACH — Basic Computer Class at Kon I Center**.

Founder name on the cover: **Keach Jock Chuol**.

## Visual direction
- Clean, modern, professional education/startup style.
- Use a simple technology + learning visual language.
- Strong readable headings, short bullets, generous spacing.
- Use simple computer, keyboard, classroom, learner and digital-skills imagery/icons where useful.
- Keep each slide visually distinct but use one consistent layout system.
- Avoid overcrowding; convert dense text into cards, diagrams, timelines or icon rows.
- Use a restrained palette with dark navy/charcoal, white and one bright accent color.
- Include a simple KEACH wordmark/logo treatment rather than inventing a complex corporate logo.

## Slide content
Use the complete copy in `pitch-deck.md` in this repository. Preserve the 12-slide order:
1. Cover
2. Problem
3. Solution
4. Target Customer
5. Market Opportunity
6. Product
7. Business Model
8. Competition
9. Go-to-Market
10. Traction / Validation
11. Team
12. Ask / Closing

## Important content rule
Do not invent market statistics, sales, users, testimonials or pilot results. The project is early-stage. The Market Opportunity slide should clearly show that local market figures are to be validated, and the Traction slide should distinguish current validation from future pilot milestones.

## Suggested visuals
- Cover: learner using a computer in a classroom.
- Problem: simple before/after digital-skills illustration.
- Solution: three or four learning-skill cards.
- Target Customer: learner profile cards.
- Market: funnel or local opportunity framework with “to validate” labels.
- Product: six-step learning journey/timeline.
- Business Model: revenue streams diagram.
- Competition: comparison matrix without ranking competitors.
- Go-to-Market: community-to-cohort flow.
- Validation: KPI dashboard with “next milestones.”
- Team: founder profile card.
- Closing: bold call to action with partnership/support icons.
