## Slide 2 — Problem
### Many graduates leave secondary school without enough practical computer skills

- Completing secondary school does not always mean being confident with computers.
- Young adults may struggle with typing, documents, email, online forms, file management and basic internet use.
- Limited digital skills can make further education, job applications and everyday tasks harder.
- Learners need practical, beginner-friendly training in a supportive environment.

**Who experiences it?**  
Secondary-school graduates who want to build useful digital skills for study, work and daily life.

---

## Slide 3 — Solution
### A practical basic-computer course at Kon I Center

KEACH provides structured, hands-on computer lessons that move learners from beginner level toward everyday digital confidence.

**Learners practice:**
- Computer basics and safe computer use
- Keyboard and mouse skills
- File and folder management
- Microsoft Word / document creation
- Basic spreadsheets
- Presentations
- Email and internet skills
- Online forms and digital services

**Outcome:** Learners leave with practical skills they can use immediately.

## Slide 6 — Product
### How the course works

**1. Register** → **2. Beginner assessment** → **3. Hands-on lessons** → **4. Practice tasks** → **5. Final skills check**

**Example learning journey**
- Week 1: Computer fundamentals
- Week 2: Typing, files and folders
- Week 3: Word processing
- Week 4: Spreadsheets and presentations
- Week 5: Email, internet and online forms
- Week 6: Practical project and assessment

**Product format**
- Small-group classroom training
- Instructor demonstrations
- Guided practice
- Individual exercises
- Practical final task

## Slide 8 — Competition
### Alternatives learners may use today

**Alternative 1: Self-learning online**
- Flexible and often free
- Can be difficult for complete beginners without guidance

**Alternative 2: Informal computer practice**
- Can provide access to computers
- May not provide a structured curriculum or assessment

**Alternative 3: Other training centers**
- May offer computer courses
- Course quality, price, schedule and accessibility can vary

**KEACH differentiation**
- Beginner-friendly
- Practical, hands-on learning
- Focused on secondary-school graduates
- Local classroom support
- Skills tied to real study, work and everyday tasks
