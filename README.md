# KEACH Pitch Deck

## Basic Computer Class at Kon I Center

**Founder:** Keach Jock Chuol

A 12-slide pitch deck for a practical basic-computer training program targeting people who have completed secondary school and want digital skills for study, work and everyday life.

### Files

- [pitch-deck.md](./pitch-deck.md) — complete 12-slide content.
- [canva-brief.md](./canva-brief.md) — design brief/prompt for creating the presentation in Canva.

### Deck structure

1. Cover
2. Problem
3. Solution
4. Target Customer
5. Market Opportunity
6. Product
7. Business Model
8. Competition
9. Go-to-Market
10. Traction / Validation
11. Team
12. Ask / Closing

The market and traction sections intentionally avoid invented statistics. Local figures and pilot results can be added once validated.
